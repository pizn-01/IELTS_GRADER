# YouTube & Shorts Scripts

## Channel positioning

**Name idea:** IELTS AI Tutor by IELTSGRADER  
**Focus:** 60-second writing fixes, band comparisons, sample walkthroughs

## Short script template (45–60 sec)

**Hook (0–3s):**
> "This Band 6 sentence is why you're stuck at 6.5."

**Problem (3–15s):**
> Show essay sentence on screen. Read it.

**Fix (15–45s):**
> Explain one criterion issue. Show improved version.

**CTA (45–60s):**
> "Get full criterion feedback free — link in bio. IELTS AI Tutor by IELTSGRADER."

## 5 ready-to-film scripts

### 1. Band 6 vs 7 intro
- Show weak intro: vague thesis
- Show Band 7 intro: clear position + roadmap
- CTA: free essay check

### 2. Task 1 trend repetition
- Show paragraph repeating "increased"
- Replace with 4 alternatives: rose, climbed, grew, surged
- CTA: Task 1 checker

### 3. Coherence fix
- Show paragraph with no topic sentence
- Add topic sentence + highlight
- CTA: 14-day plan blog post

### 4. GT formal letter opening
- Wrong: "Hey sir"
- Right: "Dear Sir or Madam,"
- CTA: mock writing test

### 5. AI tutor demo (screen record)
- Paste essay → submit → scroll report (fix cards, bands)
- Voiceover: "60 seconds, four criteria, study plan"
- CTA: ieltsgrader.com
