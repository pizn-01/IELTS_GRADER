# LinkedIn Playbook — IELTS AI Tutor (ban-safe)

**Goal:** Teach on comments; convert via **profile Website / Featured** and owned posts — never spammy comment CTAs.

## Confirmed failure mode

Commenting under another AI IELTS writing post with:

- soft CTA + raw `ieltsgrader.com`
- “Full disclosure: I'm affiliated with IELTS AI Tutor by IELTSGRADER”
- brand-looking profile avatar (“IG”)

LinkedIn returns: **“Your comment could not be created at this time.”** Do not retry that paste.

## Two surfaces

| Surface | Use for |
|---------|---------|
| **Person profile** | All comments + most discussion. Real name, educator headline. |
| **Company Page** | Owned brand posts only. Do not use Company Page to spam comments on others’ posts. |

## Identity

- **Person:** `linkedin.com/in/your-name` — headline like “IELTS Writing coach | Task 1 & 2”. Not “AI Grader / IELTSGRADER official” for the comment account.
- **Photo:** clear headshot for person; brand logo OK on Company Page only.
- **Website / Featured:** put `https://ieltsgrader.com/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=organic_profile` here — **never** in comment text.

## Comments on others’ posts

- Short professional insight only.
- **Never:** raw URLs, ieltsgrader.com, disclosure boilerplate, “free evaluation”.
- **Never:** reply-promo under competitor / other AI-tool marketing posts — skip those threads (`observe_only` in Social Ops).

## Owned posts

- **Education post:** teach only — no URL, no disclosure.
- **Soft CTA post:** say “link in my profile / Featured” — do **not** paste raw `ieltsgrader.com` in the body.

## If LinkedIn blocks a comment

1. Stop.
2. Strip links and disclosure.
3. Wait before posting again.
4. Do not retry the same promo paste.

## Measure

Profile visits and Website/Featured clicks — not comment link CTR.
