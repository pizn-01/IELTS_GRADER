# Branding — IELTS AI Tutor by IELTSGRADER

## Dual brand rule

| Context | Lead with | Secondary |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| SEO titles, meta, blog, tool pages | **IELTS AI Tutor** | by IELTSGRADER |
| App UI (Navbar, buttons, dashboard) | **IELTSGRADER** | unchanged |
| Social bios, directory listings | IELTS AI Tutor | Powered by IELTSGRADER |

## One-line positioning

> **IELTS AI Tutor by IELTSGRADER** — Your AI writing tutor that grades essays in 60 seconds, explains every mistake, and builds a personalized plan to reach your target band.

## What we are (say this)

- AI **writing tutor** for IELTS (not just a score checker)
- Grades Task 1, Task 2, Academic and General Training
- Explains **why** you lost marks (criterion-by-criterion)
- Gives fix cards, model answers, and study plans
- Mock exam mode for realistic practice
- Personalized learning editions after multiple submissions

## What we are not (avoid)

- "Just a grader" or "automated checker only"
- ChatGPT wrapper / generic AI chat
- Guaranteed band score promises
- Replacement for official IELTS registration

## Title formulas

**Homepage:** `IELTS AI Tutor — Writing Feedback & Band Score in 60 Seconds | IELTSGRADER`

**Tool pages:** `IELTS [Task] [Action] — AI Tutor Feedback | IELTSGRADER`

**Blog posts:** `[Primary keyword]: [Benefit] | IELTS AI Tutor`

**Examples:**
- `IELTS Essay Checker — AI Tutor Explains Your Band Score | IELTSGRADER`
- `Band 6 vs Band 7 Task 2: What Your AI Tutor Looks For | IELTS AI Tutor`

## Meta description formula

`[Action verb] your IELTS [task] with an AI tutor that [benefit 1], [benefit 2], and [benefit 3]. Free evaluation — no card required.`

Max ~155 characters.

## Voice

- Clear, encouraging, exam-focused
- Second person ("your essay", "your band")
- Specific (name the four criteria, task types)
- Honest about AI limits — builds trust

## Banned / cautious phrases

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| "100% accurate" | "Aligned with official IELTS band descriptors" |
| "Guaranteed Band 7" | "Personalized plan to work toward your target band" |
| "Best IELTS grader" (unsubstantiated) | "AI tutor with criterion-level feedback" |
| "Chat with your tutor" (we're not chat-first) | "Get detailed feedback and study plans" |
