# FAQ Expansion — SEO targets

Add these to tool pages and homepage FAQ schema over time.

## Scoring & accuracy
- How is my IELTS writing band score calculated?
- Is AI IELTS tutoring as good as a human examiner?
- Why did my score change on the same essay?

## Tasks & formats
- Can I check Academic Task 1 charts?
- Does the tutor grade GT formal letters?
- Can I upload handwritten essays?

## Product
- What's the difference between IELTS AI Tutor and a grader?
- How many free evaluations do I get?
- Do you offer mock writing tests?

## Study plans
- How does the 14-day sprint plan work?
- When do personalized learning editions unlock?

## Trust
- Are you affiliated with IELTS / British Council / IDP?
- Is my essay data private?

Each answer should link to a relevant tool page or blog post.
