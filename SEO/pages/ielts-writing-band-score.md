## H1: IELTS Writing Band Score Calculator (and How It Is Calculated)

Your IELTS Writing band is built from four equally weighted criteria per task, then Task 1 and Task 2 are combined with Task 2 counting double. Use the calculator for practice estimates — then submit an essay to see which criterion is capping you.

## Interactive calculator

- Task 1 + Task 2 weighted formula: (Task 1 + 2 × Task 2) ÷ 3, round to nearest 0.5
- Four-criterion average for one task (TR/TA, CC, LR, GRA)
- Practice tool only — not an official IELTS calculator

## How is the IELTS writing band score calculated?

Examiners score each task on four criteria. The task band is their average. Final Writing band ≈ (Task 1 + 2 × Task 2) ÷ 3.

Worked example for one task: TR 6, CC 7, LR 6, GRA 7 → 6.5.
