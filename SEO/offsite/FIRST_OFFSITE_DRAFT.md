**When:** Post once; reply to comments for 24–48h with more teaching.

**Title options (pick one):**
1. Why ChatGPT often gives IELTS Writing scores that feel too high
2. I compared “nice” AI essay feedback vs what examiners actually look for

**Body (copy-paste):**

I’ve been seeing a lot of people paste Task 2 essays into ChatGPT and get Band 7–8 when a human tutor would call the same essay a mid-6 / 6.5.

Here’s the pattern I keep noticing:

**1. ChatGPT rewards fluency.**  
If your English *sounds* advanced, it often bumps Lexical Resource and Grammar even when ideas are thin or the question isn’t fully answered.

**2. Examiners punish incomplete Task Response first.**  
Missing one part of a two-part question, or giving a vague opinion with no developed support, caps TR — and that usually caps the whole task. A chatbot compliment about “great vocabulary” can hide that.

**3. Coherence is about paragraph jobs, not linker count.**  
“Furthermore / Moreover / In conclusion” every three lines looks organised to a chatbot. Examiners care whether each paragraph has one clear job and logical progression.

**4. A single overall score is useless for practice.**  
If you only get “Band 7,” you don’t know whether to fix development, vocabulary precision, or grammar control. Criterion breakdown (TR/CC/LR/GRA) is what turns feedback into a plan.

**Practical check you can do today (no tools required):**
1. Underline the question parts.
2. Highlight where *each* part is answered in your essay.
3. For each body paragraph, write one margin label: “one idea only?”
4. Circle any sentence that restates the topic without adding an example or explanation.

If your essay fails step 2 or 3, treat the “Band 7” chatbot score as noise.

Happy to look at **one paragraph** (not a full essay dump) if you paste it — I’ll comment on TR vs CC specifically.
