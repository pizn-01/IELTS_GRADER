## Copy principles going forward

1. No headcounts, star ratings, or review-platform logos without a verifiable source linked from the page.
2. No official IELTS score predictions or guarantees.
3. Outcome language = *toward your target band*, never *we will get you Band X*.
4. New social proof must be tagged `[CLAIM — verify]` in drafts until approved (see `BRANDING.md`).
