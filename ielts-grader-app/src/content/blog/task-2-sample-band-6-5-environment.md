# Task 2 Sample: Band 6.5 on Environment

**Prompt:** The best way to solve environmental problems is to increase the price of fuel. Do you agree or disagree?

## Tutor verdict: 6.5

Strong Task Response and Grammar. CC and LR held the score back.

## Fix card example

**Original:** "Another problem is pollution from cars which is very bad."  
**Tutor rewrite:** "Raising fuel prices could also curb vehicle emissions, which remain a major urban pollution source."

## Lesson

One vague sentence per paragraph often costs half a band in LR and CC combined.
