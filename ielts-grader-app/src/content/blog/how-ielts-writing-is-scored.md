# How IELTS Writing Is Scored: A Tutor's Guide to the 4 Criteria

If you only look at your overall band, you miss the map your **IELTS AI Tutor** uses to help you improve. Examiners score Writing on four criteria — and your weakest one usually caps your score.

## The four criteria

### Task Achievement / Task Response
Did you answer every part of the question? Is your position clear (Task 2)? For Task 1, is there a clear overview and relevant data?

### Coherence & Cohesion
Are paragraphs logical? Do linking words connect ideas, not just fill space? Each paragraph should have one main idea.

### Lexical Resource
Range and precision matter more than rare words. Collocations and topic-appropriate vocabulary score higher than awkward "advanced" terms.

### Grammatical Range & Accuracy
Mix simple and complex sentences. Frequent errors that block meaning hurt more than occasional slips.

## How the overall band works

Your writing band is the average of the four criteria, rounded. A 6.75 average becomes 7.0; 6.25 becomes 6.5.

## What your AI tutor adds

A grader gives one number. Your tutor shows **each criterion**, fix cards for weak sentences, and a study plan targeting your lowest score.
