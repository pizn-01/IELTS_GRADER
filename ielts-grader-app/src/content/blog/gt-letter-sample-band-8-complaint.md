# GT Letter Sample: Band 8 Complaint

## Opening excerpt

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with a laptop I purchased from your store on 12 May, as it has malfunctioned repeatedly despite being under warranty.

## Why Band 8

- All bullets addressed in order
- Formal, polite, firm tone
- Clear purpose and requested action

## Tutor tip

Band 8 letters rarely waste sentences. Every line advances the request or provides necessary detail.
