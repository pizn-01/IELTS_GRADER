# Academic Task 1: Trends Without Repetition

Examiners notice when every line "increased" or "decreased." Your **IELTS AI Tutor** flags repetitive trend language in Lexical Resource.

## Rise
climbed, grew, rose, surged, recovered

## Fall
declined, dropped, fell, plunged, levelled off

## Stability
remained steady, plateaued, fluctuated around

## Practice

Describe one chart using **no repeated trend verb**. Then check with the [Task 1 checker](/ielts-task-1-checker).

## Overview reminder

One sentence: the biggest picture. Data details belong in body paragraphs.
