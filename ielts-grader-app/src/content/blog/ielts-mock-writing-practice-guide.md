# How to Practice IELTS Writing Like the Real Exam

## Weekly mock routine

1. **Day 1:** Full timed mock (60 min) on [mock writing test](/ielts-mock-writing-test)
2. **Day 1 (after):** Read AI tutor report — note lowest criterion
3. **Day 2:** Rewrite one weak paragraph only
4. **Day 4:** Short untimed drill on that criterion
5. **Day 7:** Next mock — compare same criterion band

## Timing splits

- Task 1: 20 minutes
- Task 2: 40 minutes
- 2–3 minutes planning each task

## Mistake to avoid

Untimed practice only. Stamina and time allocation are skills.

## After the mock

Your tutor report includes fix cards and a study plan — use them the same day while the essay is fresh.
