# From Feedback to Study Plan

Your **IELTS AI Tutor** report is not the end — it is the syllabus for your next two weeks.

## Step 1: Find the floor
Note your lowest criterion (not just overall band).

## Step 2: One skill per week
Week A: Coherence drills. Week B: Lexical precision. Do not fix everything at once.

## Step 3: Rewrite, don't reread
Take one fix card from your report. Rewrite that paragraph in a new essay within 48 hours.

## Step 4: Re-submit
Same task type, new question. Compare criterion bands in your dashboard.

## Step 5: Unlock learning editions
After multiple essays, personalized learning editions summarize your recurring errors.
