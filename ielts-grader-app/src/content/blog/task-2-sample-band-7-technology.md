# Task 2 Sample: Band 7 Essay on Technology

**Prompt:** Technology has made it easier for people to work from home. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

## Sample introduction (Band 7)

> Remote working has become increasingly common thanks to digital communication tools. While this shift offers clear benefits in flexibility and reduced commuting, it also raises concerns about collaboration and work-life boundaries. Overall, I believe the advantages outweigh the drawbacks when employers provide adequate structure.

## Why this scores Band 7

- **Clear position** in the final sentence
- **Both sides** acknowledged before the opinion
- **Topic-specific vocabulary:** remote working, commuting, collaboration

## Tutor's note for Band 8

Add a more nuanced concession in body paragraph 2 and vary sentence openings further.
