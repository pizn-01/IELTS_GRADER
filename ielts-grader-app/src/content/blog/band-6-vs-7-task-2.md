# Band 6 vs Band 7 Task 2: What Your AI Tutor Looks For

The jump from 6 to 7 is rarely about "better grammar." Your **IELTS AI Tutor** most often flags these gaps:

## 1. Under-developed ideas (Task Response)

**Band 6:** States a point, moves on.  
**Band 7:** Explains, exemplifies, and links back to the question.

## 2. Paragraph control (Coherence)

**Band 6:** Topic sentences are vague.  
**Band 7:** Each paragraph has one job, opened with a clear topic sentence.

## 3. Precision over rarity (Lexical Resource)

**Band 6:** "big problem", "very important"  
**Band 7:** "pressing issue", "fundamental priority"

## 4. Controlled complexity (Grammar)

**Band 7** writers use complex sentences when they add clarity — not to impress.
