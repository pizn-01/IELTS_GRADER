# Is AI IELTS Tutoring Accurate?

Students ask whether an **IELTS AI Tutor** can replace human feedback. Here is an honest answer.

## What AI tutors do well

- Fast criterion-by-criterion breakdown
- Consistent application of band descriptors
- Pattern tracking across many essays
- Unlimited practice at low cost

## Limitations

- Not an official IELTS examiner
- May miss subtle pragmatics or highly creative phrasing
- Cannot assess speaking or reading

## How IELTS AI Tutor by IELTSGRADER approaches accuracy

We align feedback to public band descriptors and show **why** each criterion scored as it did — fix cards, model answers, study plans.

## Best practice

Use AI tutoring for frequent practice; use human tutors periodically for calibration before your test date.
