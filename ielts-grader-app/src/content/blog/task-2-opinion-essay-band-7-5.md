# Task 2 Opinion Essay: Band 7.5 Sample

**Prompt:** Some people think governments should spend money on railways rather than roads. To what extent do you agree?

## Thesis (Band 7.5)

> Although road investment remains necessary for rural connectivity, I largely agree that public funds should prioritise rail because it reduces congestion and emissions in dense urban corridors.

## Tutor breakdown

- **TR 7.5:** Clear, qualified position
- **CC 7:** Logical progression; one transition could be tighter
- **LR 7.5:** Topic vocabulary: congestion, emissions, corridors
- **GRA 7.5:** Mixed structures with few errors

## One fix for Band 8

Add a specific example (e.g. a city that shifted commuters from road to rail).
