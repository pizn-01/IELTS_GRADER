# 14-Day Plan to Improve Coherence & Cohesion

Your **IELTS AI Tutor** often marks Coherence & Cohesion lowest when essays "feel fine" but paragraphs wander. This sprint fixes that.

## Days 1–3: Topic sentences
Write 5 topic sentences for common Task 2 topics. Each must state one main idea only.

## Days 4–6: Linking that connects
Rewrite 3 paragraphs replacing generic "Moreover" with links that reference the previous idea.

## Days 7–9: Full essays (timed 40 min)
Two Task 2 essays. After each, highlight the first sentence of every paragraph — do they map your argument?

## Days 10–12: Task 1 overview practice
Write overviews only for 3 charts. Check: one clear trend + no data list.

## Days 13–14: Mock + review
Take a [mock writing test](/ielts-mock-writing-test). Focus edits on CC comments in your tutor report.

## Track progress
Re-submit one essay on day 1 and day 14 — compare CC band in your report.
