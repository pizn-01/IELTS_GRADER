# IELTS Task 2 Sample: Band 6 Essay on Education

**Prompt:** Some people believe university education should be free for everyone. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## Sample essay (Band 6)

> University education is very important for students. Many people think it should be free because education is a right. I partly agree with this idea.
>
> Free university can help poor students study. If education is expensive, only rich people can go to university. This is not fair for society.
>
> However, free education costs a lot of money for the government. The money might be needed for hospitals and roads. Also, when university is free, some students may not study hard.
>
> In conclusion, I think university should be cheaper but not completely free. Government and students should share the cost.

## AI tutor feedback

| Criterion | Band | Note |
|-----------|------|------|
| Task Response | 6 | Position is clear but ideas are thin |
| Coherence | 6 | Structure OK; body paragraphs need development |
| Lexical Resource | 6 | Repetition: "free", "university", "education" |

**Top fix:** Develop body paragraphs with specific examples (e.g. Nordic funding models, scholarship alternatives).
