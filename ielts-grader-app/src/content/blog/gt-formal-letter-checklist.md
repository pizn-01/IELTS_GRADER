# GT Formal Letter Checklist

Your **IELTS AI Tutor** checks letters against bullet points and tone. Use this before every GT Task 1.

## Opening
- "Dear Sir or Madam," when name unknown
- State purpose in sentence one

## Bullet coverage
- One paragraph per bullet (or logical grouping)
- Do not invent bullets not in the prompt

## Tone
- Formal: no contractions, no slang
- Semi-formal: polite but warmer

## Closing
- "Yours faithfully," (Sir/Madam) or "Yours sincerely," (named person)

## Common Band 6 mistake
Wrong tone — informal chat in a complaint letter.
