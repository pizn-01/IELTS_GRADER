# Task 1 Bar Chart Sample Answer Band 7 with Tutor Notes

Academic Task 1 rewards a clear overview, accurate data selection, and controlled comparisons, not a catalogue of every bar. This **Band 7** sample from **IELTS AI Tutor by IELTSGRADER** shows a full report with tutor notes on what examiners look for.

## The task (practice prompt)

The bar chart below shows the percentage of households in three countries (A, B, and C) that owned a car in 2000, 2010, and 2020.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

### Data table (for practice without an image)

| Country | 2000 | 2010 | 2020 |
|---------|------|------|------|
| Country A | 40% | 55% | 70% |
| Country B | 65% | 68% | 72% |
| Country C | 25% | 45% | 60% |

## Full sample report (Band 7)

The bar chart compares car ownership among households in three countries in 2000, 2010, and 2020.

Overall, car ownership rose in all three countries over the period, but the scale of change differed. Country A and Country C saw substantial growth, whereas Country B started highest and increased only gradually. By 2020, Country A and Country B had similarly high ownership rates, while Country C remained the lowest despite clear improvement.

In 2000, Country B had the highest proportion of households with a car, at 65%, compared with 40% in Country A and just 25% in Country C. Over the next decade, Country A climbed to 55% and Country C rose sharply to 45%, narrowing the gap with Country B, which edged up only slightly to 68%.

By 2020, ownership in Country A had reached 70%, almost matching Country B at 72%. Country C continued its upward trend to 60%, yet it still lagged behind the other two. The most striking feature is therefore the rapid catch-up in Countries A and C, contrasted with the relative stability of already-high ownership in Country B.

*(Word count: ~178)*

## Tutor annotations

### Overview (do not skip this)

> Overall, car ownership rose in all three countries… Country A and Country C saw substantial growth, whereas Country B… increased only gradually.

**Why Band 7:** The overview states the big picture without listing every figure. Missing or overview-as-detail is a common Task Achievement cap at Band 6.

### Data selection

The report does **not** narrate every cell in isolation. It groups:

- Starting ranking in 2000
- Mid-period movement (especially C’s sharp rise)
- End ranking and the “catch-up” story

That selection supports Task Achievement.

### Comparison language

> almost matching Country B at 72%  
> still lagged behind the other two  
> relative stability of already-high ownership

Comparisons are the point of a multi-category bar chart. See more options in [trends vocabulary](/blog/task-1-trends-vocabulary).

### What the report wisely avoids

- No causes (*because incomes rose*)
- No opinions (*this is good for society*)
- No mechanical listing (*In 2000 A was 40. In 2010 A was 55…*) without synthesis

## Fix cards if your Task 1 is stuck at Band 6

### Fix card 1: Write the overview before the details

After reading the chart for one minute, write two overview sentences with **no more than one number** (or none). Then write details.

### Fix card 2: Group by story, not by year only

Instead of “2000 paragraph, 2010 paragraph, 2020 paragraph” with no thread, group by *who changed most* vs *who stayed high*.

### Fix card 3: Replace repeated *increase*

Use *climb, rise, edge up, grow* and noun forms (*growth, upward trend*) where accurate. Keep degree words honest (*slightly* vs *sharply*).

## Band 6 vs Band 7 mini contrast (opening)

**Band 6:**  
> The chart shows car ownership in three countries. Country A was 40% in 2000, 55% in 2010 and 70% in 2020. Country B was 65%, 68% and 72%. Country C was 25%, 45% and 60%. Ownership increased.

**Band 7:**  
> The chart compares household car ownership in three countries across 2000–2020. Overall, ownership increased everywhere, with the strongest growth in Countries A and C, while Country B remained highest throughout but rose only marginally.

## Timing checklist (20 minutes)

| Minutes | Action |
|---------|--------|
| 0–3 | Read axes, units, categories; note max/min and biggest change |
| 3–5 | Write overview (2 sentences) |
| 5–16 | Two body paragraphs with selected figures + comparisons |
| 16–20 | Check units, tense, and overview presence |
